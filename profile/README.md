<h1 align="center">BeMomin</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Become the Muslim you want to be, one day at a time.</b></p>

BeMomin helps a Muslim build the habits of the deen through tracking, structure and timely reminders. Salah, Quran, adhkar, duas and tasks all feed one picture of the day, so a late night, a missed Fajr and a slipped task are seen together, and tomorrow starts better.

## Try it

The app is in private testing. Visit [bemomin.com](https://bemomin.com) to learn more.

## What is in the app

- **Salah**: log each prayer in one tap, with jamaah and takbeer, and see your week.
- **Quran**: a verified Uthmani reader that remembers where you stopped.
- **Adhkar and duas**: morning and evening adhkar, masnoon duas with sources, your own collections.
- **Tasks**: plan the day around your prayers.
